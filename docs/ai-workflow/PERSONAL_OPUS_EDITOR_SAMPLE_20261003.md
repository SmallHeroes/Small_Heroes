## Inferences and proposed next milestone

There is no instruction forcing one location. Existing instructions explicitly
allow a rewritten outline and ask for causal movement, discovery and no filler.
Possible pressures are the per-spread planning checklist, generic age guidance,
mandatory failed attempt and soft word targets. Their individual causality is
not established by this one sample.

Recommended future correction is within existing authoring stages: separate
backstage state from narrative voice; give concrete age-sensitive craft guidance;
distinguish meaningful development from stretching one task; require the editor
to examine adjacent spreads and the dramatized payoff, not merely enumerate
functions. Keep continuity, facts, exclusions, companion abilities and bindings.
Do not add a mandatory location/joke/obstacle quota or a rewrite loop. This is a
proposal, not an implemented or independently reviewed change.
