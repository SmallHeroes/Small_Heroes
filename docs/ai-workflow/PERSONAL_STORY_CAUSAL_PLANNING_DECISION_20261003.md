# Personal story causal planning decision

Recommendation for Guy: strengthen the existing planner and editor instead of
adding another story engine. The six-book trial showed that exact citations and
self-audits do not protect meaning: independent comparison found new causal damage
in Tom/Yoav and a weakened companion contribution in Alma. GPT's feedback agrees
that the main gap is preparation of the adventure and preservation of its logic,
not further sentence polish. This is a proposal, not implementation or spending
authorization. Claude's technical regression fixes are a separate milestone.
Revision after Guy forwarded GPT's planning feedback: this document remains a
pending Decision Gate. The feedback is not new implementation or paid-run approval.

## 1 Proposed change

Start with the quality of the idea, not merely its logical feasibility. Retain
the two-concept comparison, using its existing curiosity, discovery, child
contribution and payoff fields to explain why a child would want to hear the next
part and what makes the ending worth reaching. Changing scenery or props alone
does not establish a different adventure. A model's reason is retained evidence
of its choice, NOT proof that either concept is interesting.

Allow the planner to decline both concepts explicitly instead of being forced to
select A or B. In the experimental path only, propose at most ONE further planning
attempt per profile, with a recorded reason and a fully reserved slot before any
dispatch. A second rejection holds that profile before authorship. Transport,
schema, source-binding and budget errors do not retry through this allowance.
This is a bounded idea-selection experiment, not an
open rewrite loop or permission to reuse the consumed cohort family.

For an admitted concept, the planner describes the whole adventure as a bounded
continuous synopsis, then breaks it into spreads. In that planning response it
records a short backward dependency check of the climax: what changed, what the
child noticed or tried, what enables action, what the companion contributes, and
which established desire or relationship the ending pays off. Dependencies point
to actual planned beats; no new facts about the real child are invented.

These are questions, not a mandatory plot. A story need not have a timer, a broken
object, a companion mistake or a mechanical rescue. Where there is a time limit,
its trigger and consequence must be clear; the prompt must not presuppose one.
Retain two genuinely different concepts and meaningful personal facts. Do not
turn the continuous synopsis into a list of compliance claims.

The editor records an original-text diagnosis with evidence, repair scope
(wording/scene/structure), and UP TO THREE substantial strengths with their narrative
functions. It can preserve wording or improve it, but must account for changed
functions and dependent setup/payoff. Keep coherent intentional revision possible;
do not make fictional rules immutable or force exactly three faults or strengths.
Zero strengths is valid; do not manufacture praise to satisfy an array minimum.
Strengths describe source-backed functions worth preserving, not quality approval.
Each retained strength points to original prose and its narrative function; the
edited result records how that function was kept or coherently changed. Do not
force literal wording or a weak magical rule to survive a justified revision.

## 2 Why now

Current plans already include goals, companion wants, humour, action/consequence,
continuity and an ending; selection has three model outline checks. There is no
continuous synopsis or explicit climax dependency record. The editor reads the
whole original and returns final comparison citations, but has no recorded
pre-edit diagnosis/strengths. More citation checks alone will not close these gaps.

Important distinction: the GPT experimental runtime already calls plan before
manuscript. The native Opus trial combined plan and manuscript in one invocation.
For a real pre-prose boundary, the next Opus experiment should split planning
from original authorship, then use a fresh editor process. A JSON field order or
an instruction to "diagnose first" cannot prove the model's internal order.
The editor diagnosis can remain in its one response initially; a separately
approved diagnosis before rewrite would need another paid stage.

## 3 Scope and hardcoding risk

General rules tested in the diagnostic path across approved fictional profiles
and companions, not a production-wide cutover.
No plot repairs specific to Tom, Yoav, Alma, ducks, wind, balloons or clocks.
Current residence/starting-place distinction, exact approved facts, character
abilities, grammatical address, selected length and no-dash prose remain.
The wizard, landing design, legacy catalog, payments and image renderer do not change.

## 5 Expected behavior and acceptance

The admitted full-story plan precedes the manuscript invocation. A structural
failure/HOLD makes no downstream authorship/editor call; only the explicitly
reserved one-time planning reattempt above can follow an idea rejection. In a
cohort trial, collect and read the admitted synopses TOGETHER before writing any
manuscript. Hold repetitive/weak proposals before prose rather than discovering
the cohort pattern after six books. This review does not create a memory system,
cross-customer context or permanent diversity quotas.

The writer gets approved facts and the selected plan, not old prose or
self-congratulation. The fresh editor gets the complete
original and brief, not the author's chat. Author and editor both remain Opus;
Astra remains the independent literary critic.

Evaluate the ORIGINAL on its own in a fresh critic context, and the FINAL in
another fresh context. Both receive the same approved brief, including age,
companion authority, length, facts, topic/exclusions and resilience mode. Neither
receives editor ratings, selection justifications or the other version. Freeze
these readings before original/final comparison; original assessment is not
silently fed back into this trial's editor. Existing `story-text-review` final
packet already allowlists this brief; add an original-only sibling, not a rating
field to the writer result. Comparison then records what improved, remained
unresolved or was damaged, citing both versions. Each packet/report binds its
actual source. This does not self-award independent technical PASS.

Source-bound citations prove only provenance. A plan's explanation can still be
wrong. Quality acceptance requires reading the actual original/final stories;
none of this guarantees a perfect book. The current runtime has no independent
literary QA dispatch: existing packets are offline advisory inputs. A product
QA gate needs a separately bounded adapter/accounting milestone before rollout.

## 6 Validation plan

First free tests, in separate milestones: trusted current-admission downgrade
rejection with archive reads preserved; then accepted/rejected concept states,
bounded planning slots, no prose before cohort-plan review, synopsis/dependency
coverage, unknown fact rejection, valid setup/payoff references, unchanged
character authority, zero-to-three strength/diagnosis bindings, refusal of stale
packets, and independent original/final critic inputs. References and source
quotes do NOT establish that a claimed causal link is true or a story is exciting.
Mutation tests must prove each clause independently. Claude Code reviews the
committed range before paid measurement.

Then a separately costed, newly authorized, single-use text experiment on known
and unseen profiles, all six companions and mixed8/12/16 spreads. Include a
profile with no chosen difficulty, one with sparse but sufficient approved facts,
and two with the same hobby but different stated habits/wants. Do not invent extra
biographical facts to make a sparse case richer or enforce a full factorial matrix.
Keep both concepts, every planning attempt, synopsis, original and edit. Before
prose, cohort review compares wants, complications, discoveries and solutions;
report repetition without a location
quota or forbidding naturally recurring emotional choices. Do not leak another
child's profile/manuscript into an author's input to manufacture diversity.

Measure three questions separately: original quality under old versus improved
planning, original-to-edit gains/damage, and final quality under each path. At a
minimum independently assess originals as well as edits. For a controlled old/new
comparison, match profiles, models, lengths and other conditions; historical
books alone are qualitative references, not a controlled comparison. If several
features change together, call the result a package comparison, not proof of which
feature helped. Compare curiosity, surprise, causal clarity, character voice,
humour and satisfying payoff, not just compliance or scores. Guy's reading and
read-aloud feedback remain the product quality decision.
