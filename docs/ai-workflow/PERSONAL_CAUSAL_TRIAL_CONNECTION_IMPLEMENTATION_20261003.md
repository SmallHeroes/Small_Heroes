## Error and HOLD semantics

Host seals on transport/schema/cost/binding/source/journal failure, cancellation
or stage deadline, including errors after transport in coordinator compilation.
Late replies cannot mutate settled accounting or authorize another call.
Billed incomplete/schema failures can retain known usage in the HOST journal;
the unchanged coordinator's failure receipt may still have unknown cost. These
layers are not conflated. Fixed failure codes are exposed, not provider errors.

Planning outline/both-rejected HOLD cannot receive write approval; explicit
synopsis hold skips that case. Literary editor/critic HOLD is diagnostic data:
the reviewed coordinator still finishes its three advisory readings. It does
not award product PASS, release or runtime eligibility. The proposal's "HOLD
stop" means technical/admission refusal, not suppressing independent examination
of a structurally valid but weak edited book. This distinction is made explicit
without silently rewriting the independently reviewed coordinator.
