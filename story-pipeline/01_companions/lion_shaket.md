# Companion Story Bible · ליאו האריה (`lion_shaket`)

**Status:** next-generation staging canon; not runtime authority.

**Challenge category:** `ANGER_FRUSTRATION` · **Gender:** male · **Display name:** ליאו.

**Immutable visual identity:** small gentle lion cub, large emotionally alert eyes, soft rounded ears, full rounded fluffy mane with the same size/shape on every page; no canonical accessory and no neck item.

## Child-facing promise

ליאו has a roar larger than his body and an increasingly complicated habit of trying to store it in containers that were not designed for weather. His mane announces incoming thunder before he does. He is never violent or ashamed; he wants the important thing fixed and initially mistakes “put the roar somewhere” for a complete plan.

## Story engine

- **Visible desire:** repair an unfair interruption, recover a ruined plan, protect a turn, complete a build, or make an important objection heard.
- **Lovable flaw:** he tries to make a strong objection so tidy and polite that its physical energy leaks into everything nearby.
- **Wrong help:** packs, parks, whispers, or redirects the near-roar into an unsuitable object/place; the object wobbles, rattles, expands, rolls away, or passes the rumble onward.
- **Embodied tell:** mane puffs, quivers, or becomes asymmetrically enormous before his voice arrives; paws grip the ground while he claims everything is under control.
- **Child-only ability:** the child identifies what mattered in the visible situation and uses a clear action/request/design change to address it, instead of only moving the rumble.
- **Relationship change:** ליאו gives honest energy and refuses to pretend nothing happened; the child gives that energy a purpose in the plot. He follows the child's chosen repair.
- **Emotional underlayer, never headline:** anger can signal that something mattered, but expression alone does not repair the thing.

## Comic engine — “the roar escapes the wrong container”

1. ליאו selects a container/place far too small or ceremoniously unsuitable for his near-roar.
2. His mane reveals the pressure while he insists the arrangement is excellent.
3. The rumble escapes through a physical chain reaction and makes the visible problem stranger.

Escalate from one rattling object, to a moving set piece, to a final release that exposes what action is actually needed. The humor is in disproportion and dignity, never in mocking anger.

## Voice and oral Hebrew

- Direct, warm, dignified, slightly ceremonial when choosing ridiculous containers.
- Can be emphatic without shouting or threatening.
- Sample voice, not mandatory catchphrases: “שמתי את הרעם בכיס. הכיס מבקש לעבור למכנסיים אחרים.” · “הרעמה שלי מגזימה. אני—ררר—מסכים חלקית.” · “לא צריך שאגה גדולה. צריך שהגשר ייפתח.”
- No “calm down,” anger validation lecture, adult psychology, or moral about good/bad anger.

## Play vocabulary

- **Actions:** brace paws, redirect rolling vibration, repair, rebuild, state a concrete objection, choose a useful outlet/action, follow a rumbling trail.
- **Objects:** tins, pockets, drums without sticks, paper tubes, carts, bridges, flags, blocks, containers that comically transmit vibration.
- **World affordances:** construction sites, races, parades, workshops, bridges, hill paths, thunder orchards, cloud ports; bedroom/pillow fort is not the default.
- **Reread hooks:** mane pressure visible before dialogue; a small opening object returning as the final useful signal; rumble moving through backgrounds.

## Direction modulation

- **Bedtime:** an evening repair or last-route problem begins energetic, then the final quarter converts scattered rumble into one precise, low-energy action and an earned quiet aftermath. No bed or “anger corner” is required.
- **Adventure:** frustration repeatedly disrupts a moving task; child redirects effort into the decisive repair or fair rule.
- **Fantasy:** thunder obeys one physical rule across multiple set pieces; no battle, cathartic scream climax, or abstract anger landscape.

## Do not write

- violent, threatening, dominant, or courage-symbol lion;
- “anger is okay” lecture, “anger means something mattered” as narrated moral, or regulation worksheet;
- swallowing the roar as virtue or explosive shouting as cure;
- mandatory pillow cave, bedroom thunder corner, paws/mane/place mantra, or old V5 plot residue;
- mane changing size/shape as identity drift—comic puffing must preserve the canonical silhouette;
- ליאו fixing the injustice/obstacle instead of the child;
- cape, scarf, bow, held identity prop, or any neck accessory.

## Swap test and acceptance

Replacing ליאו with a generic angry animal must break the near-roar/container physics, mane tells, dignified wrong choices, rumble trail, and child conversion of energy into a concrete repair.

- [ ] A visible important plan is disrupted; anger is not the plot by itself.
- [ ] Three wrong containers/locations create different physical consequences.
- [ ] The near-roar never becomes a frightening full-force explosion.
- [ ] The child identifies and performs the useful repair/request.
- [ ] The ending resolves the external problem without summarizing an anger lesson.

## Visual continuity locks

- Small gentle cub; full rounded fluffy mane remains same canonical size and shape in every view.
- Mane may quiver/puff internally but never become sparse, absent, flat, or a different silhouette.
- No accessory, cape, scarf, bow, held hero prop, or neck item.
- Keep warm fur separated from warm lighting and avoid menacing teeth/poses.
- Honest near-roar is expressive, not aggressive.
