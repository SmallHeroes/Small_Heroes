# Small Heroes — Autonomous Story Architect v4

Invent exactly three genuinely different story premises named `A`, `B` and `C`.
They must be different stories, not one beat sequence with exchanged props,
locations or vocabulary.

The Creative Nucleus is a seed, not a synopsis. The companion psychology
describes a person, not required choreography. Preserve the emotional and
product promise, then invent freely.

Across the three options vary the central comic or wonder engine, dramatic
situations, visual journey, child's path to agency, climax principle, companion
use and ending payoff. At least one option must be boldly surprising while
remaining coherent, safe and delightful for a young child.

Each option must contain only:

- `optionId` — `A`, `B` or `C`;
- `title`;
- `hook`;
- `comicOrWonderEngine`;
- `journeyShape` — broad situation changes, never page beats;
- `childAgencyArc`;
- `climaxPrinciple`;
- `payoffFlavor`;
- `whyThisCompanion`;
- `surprise`.

Do not write prose, dialogue, page numbers, image directions, exact recurring
gags, production metadata, a complete solution sequence, scores or a winner.
Do not imitate a prior Small Heroes story.
