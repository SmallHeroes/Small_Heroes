# Small Heroes — Musical Read-Aloud Polish Charter v1

**Status:** staging-only language-polish authority.

Polish the supplied Hebrew story for pleasurable oral reading without changing
the story. The input has already passed structural editorial review; preserve
its dramatic work instead of inventing a new version of the plot.

## What may change

- sentence length, punctuation and line breaks;
- natural rhythmic phrasing and varied cadence;
- selective internal or end rhyme where it arrives without strain;
- sound-play, onomatopoeia and consonant/vowel patterning;
- short rhythmic repetition that strengthens comedy, motion, tension or a page
  turn; and
- a page ending whose spoken cadence better invites the next page.

## What must not change

- title, identity frontmatter, page count or page order;
- story events, causal mechanism, discovery sequence or dramatic function;
- the child's agency, experiment, decision or climax ownership;
- the companion's actions, choices, adaptation or relationship to the child;
- locations, important objects, visual-comedy set pieces, payoff or ending;
- gender meaning or personalization syntax; or
- any fact merely to obtain a rhyme.

## Musicality boundary

Do not turn the whole story into a poem. There is no rhyme quota and no required
refrain. Never use filler, inverted syntax, an unnatural synonym, a slogan,
forced diminutive or sing-song repetition merely to complete a rhyme. Keep
emotionally clear and causally important moments in natural prose when rhyme
would weaken them.

Do not make a rhyme depend on only one side of a gender chip. Every chip must
contain two complete Hebrew forms: `{boy-form|girl-form}`.

Return one complete story, not a patch, analysis or revision summary. Preserve
the minimal frontmatter and numbered page format exactly. Add no image
directions, production metadata or commentary.
