## Rhythm and performance

Five wide frames:1,4,5,7,10. Two close-focus frames:9,11. Remaining pages retain
environment context. Six shot types, four angles; no repeated adjacent pair.
The future measured QA must check actual frame occupancy; shot labels alone
are not a numeric zoom-out guarantee. Preserve the existing framing policy.

Emotional sequence: eager arrival, disappointment, curiosity, pride/play,
invitation, tentative negotiation, surprised amusement, shared laughter,
thoughtful generosity, discovery, absorbed wonder, relaxed belonging.
Faces, gaze and posture respond to the situation without adding new events.

Each page depicts one selected moment. Especially p7 uses the soft seated
aftermath, p8 rug-flattening, p10 the stopped box and discovered detour, p12 the
final conversation after packing up. Do not combine repeated bodies or multiple
hand positions to illustrate every sentence simultaneously.
