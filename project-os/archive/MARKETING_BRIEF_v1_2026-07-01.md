## 0. What changed vs. the ChatGPT brief (read this first)
The ChatGPT brief is a strong *generic* playbook. This version bends it to four facts it ignored:

1. **The bottleneck is not demand — it's throughput × Guy's QA.** Marketing targets are now capped to what we can actually fulfil at book quality. Over-selling breaks the QA gate and produces exactly the "beautiful but not sellable" failure we're guarding against.
2. **Launch = 2026-07-15 (14 days out), not a blank 90-day canvas.** The calendar below is anchored to that date and to the 18-slot / PayMe / Cursor+Guy-only reality.
3. **Discount + referral + influencer commission must be costed before promised.** Margin guardrails added.
4. **"Don't lead with AI" ≠ hide AI.** Honest stance codified so the keepsake doesn't become a backlash.

## 2. Positioning (kept — it's good)
- Core line: **"הספר שבו הילד שלכם באמת הגיבור."**
- Differentiation: **"לא עוד ספר עם שם — עולם שלם שנוצר סביב הילד."**
- Emotional-first, premium keepsake, meaningful gift. **Do NOT lead with tech.**
- Banned words: מהפכני, מטורף, הכי טוב בעולם, AI מתקדם, ג'נרטיבי, אוטומציה, "העתיד של ספרי הילדים".
- Preferred: אישי, גיבור, סיפור שנשאר, מתנה עם לב, מזכרת, דמיון, אומץ, לפני השינה, הילד שלכם בתוך הסיפור.
- **Voice:** warm, Israeli, personal, simple, trustworthy, premium-not-pretentious. No hype, few exclamation marks.

### Honesty stance (new — binding)
Emotional-first, but never dishonest. Concretely:
- **Don't promise "in minutes."** Say "אנחנו יוצרים ומגיהים כל ספר בקפידה" — quality takes time.
- **Don't promise visual resemblance without a photo.** If no photo: allowed to proceed, but state the limitation.
- **If asked directly "is this AI?"** — answer honestly and reframe on the human curation/QA layer ("כן, ובזה בדיוק הערך: כל ספר עובר בקרה אישית לפני שהוא יוצא"). Don't dodge.

## 3. The throttled "circles" plan, mapped to the calendar

### Phase A — Pre-launch (now → 07-15, 14 days)
Goal: **be ready to sell, not to broadcast.** No public campaign yet.
- Lock **2–3 strong demo books** across different children (boy/girl, different looks) — this is the single most important marketing asset and it doubles as a QA proof.
- Landing page essentials live: hero line, "how it works", **real page spreads** (not mockups), FAQ, **privacy / child-photo policy**, one clear CTA. WhatsApp Business ready with saved replies + price/timeline/delivery.
- Draft the pilot outreach list: **personal network first** (family, friends, gan parents, neighbors, Odem/Golan community). Aim ~40–60 names, not 100 — you only need enough to fill a *throttled* pilot.
- Prepare (don't publish) ~6–8 pieces of content from the demo books.

### Phase B — Launch pilot (07-15 → ~08-05, ~3 weeks): **"מחפשים 10–15 משפחות ראשונות"**
> Deliberately smaller than ChatGPT's "30". 30 orders at manual QA is a capacity trap. Start at **10–15 total**, sized to ~3–5 QA'd books/week.
- Open only to Circle 1 (personal + local). **No FB-group blast yet.**
- Offer: launch price + honest feedback + optional testimonial/photo permission + share-with-2.
- The ask back: (1) feedback, (2) reaction photo/video if comfortable, (3) permission to use, (4) refer 2 relevant parents.
- **Success = quality proof, not volume:** 10–15 delighted families, 5+ usable reaction videos, 8–10 testimonials.

### Phase C — Social proof → controlled expansion (~08-05 → ~09-15)
- Turn real reactions into content (child-reaction reels = #1 asset).
- Open **Circle 2** (local FB/WhatsApp parent groups) with the *community/pilot* framing, not an ad. **Only widen intake as QA capacity allows** — keep a waitlist if demand > capacity.
- Recruit **3–5 micro-influencers** (2k–20k, Israeli moms/parenting/gan). Free personalized book + follower discount code; commission only if margin allows (see §4).
- Begin **1–2 kindergarten/daycare** conversations as trust channels.

### Phase D — Paid readiness (only after C proves out)
Do NOT start paid before: landing converts, ~20 testimonials, ~10 real videos, clear top buyer, stable price/delivery, 2–3 proven organic creatives. Paid amplifies proven content — it doesn't guess.

## 4. Pricing & margin guardrails (new — must resolve before promising stacked offers)
- **Launch prices:** bedtime ₪59 · adventure ₪79 · fantasy ₪99 (≈50% launch discount off future price).
- **The stacking trap:** ~50% launch discount **+** referral credit **+** 15% influencer commission can compound to a negative contribution margin.
- **Guardrail:** never stack all three on one order. Pick a lane per channel:
  - Personal pilot → launch price only.
  - Referral → friend 10% off, buyer gets credit *only redeemable on a 2nd order* (keeps first-order margin intact).
  - Influencer → free book (CAC) OR follower discount OR commission — **one**, not all.
- *Open numbers to fill: unit COGS (gpt-image-2 HIGH render + narration + print/ship if printed) and target contribution margin at launch price.* Until costed, treat every stacked offer as a defect.

## 5. Content plan — sized for one founder
Forget "4 Reels/week + 20 posts + 10 videos in week 1" — that's a team. Solo reality:
- **Must-have:** authentic child-reaction video from real pilot families (Phase B). Everything else is secondary.
- **Sustainable cadence (Phase B/C):** 2–3 short vertical videos/week (reuse across IG Reels / TikTok / YT Shorts) + 1–2 stills/week + near-daily Stories from real moments.
- **Formats that matter first:** (1) book-opening reaction, (2) "not just a name" comparison, (3) grandparent-gift angle, (4) fast page-flip of a real spread.
- Defer photographer/editor/VA until there's revenue to justify them.

## 8. Risks & gates
- **R1 (top): demand > QA capacity.** Mitigation: throttled intake + public waitlist. A waitlist is *good* social proof; a broken delivery promise is fatal.
- **R2: margin erosion from stacked offers.** Mitigation: §4 guardrail + cost the unit first.
- **R3: honesty/AI backlash.** Mitigation: §2 honesty stance.
- **R4: child-photo privacy trust.** Mitigation: explicit policy on landing (what's stored, why, how long, deletion). Non-negotiable before any group posting.
- **R5: solo content burnout.** Mitigation: §5 cadence, real-moment content over produced content.
